Step 1: Kid Speech / Text Input

Phase 1

A child asks a question by tapping the Glowing Orb (Web Speech Recognition API) or typing in the chatbox. Supports natural accents and speech across 12 languages (English, Tamil, Spanish, Korean, Japanese, Hindi, etc.).

Speech To Text Stream ⬇️
🌐🔤

Step 2: Language Detector & Intent Router

Phase 2

The AgentEngine normalizes the text and detects dynamic language switch requests (e.g. "Speak in Tamil" or "Habla en español"). It then classifies the inquiry into specialized domains: Math, Science & Astronomy, Storytelling, Riddles, or Timer.

Routed to Specialized Engine ⬇️
⚙️🧠

Step 3: Five Specialized Intelligence Cores

Phase 3

The inquiry is dispatched to the corresponding specialized core:

🌌 AI Reasoning Core
Calculates celestial distances (Earth-Moon ~384,400 km), gravity, speed of light, and physics principles.
🔢 Math & Logic Engine
Solves multi-step arithmetic, fractions, sequence patterns (2, 4, 8, 16 ➔ 32), and word problems.
📖 Story & Quiz Core
Loads short, medium, or long stories and evaluates comprehension answers with star badges.
🌐 Live Web Fallback
Real-time Wikipedia Encyclopedia lookup for curiosity questions outside the curated base.
4-Step AI Reasoning Deduction ⬇️
💭📐

Step 4: Transparent 4-Step Thinking Process

Phase 4

Before formulating the final answer, the AI generates a transparent 4-stage thinking trace for the child:
1. 🎯 Goal Identification (What is being asked?)
2. 🔍 Fact Retrieval (What laws/constants apply?)
3. 📐 Reasoning & Verification (Checking numbers, metric vs. imperial units)
4. 💡 Kid Formulation (Converting to inspiring analogies, e.g. "30 Earths in a row")

Voice Pitch & Rate Modulation ⬇️
🧒👧🗣️

Step 5: Distinct Kids Voice Synthesis

Phase 5

The VoiceEngine applies individual vocal personality curves (pitch from 1.35x to 1.70x and calibrated speed) for boys and girls (Leo Junior, Charlie, Toby, Emma, Maya, Lily).

Render to Screen & Audio Speaker ⬇️
⭐🎉📱

Step 6: Rich Interactive Card & Star Reward

Phase 6

The UI renders the animated thinking card, key metric pills, and celebratory audio chimes. Star badges (⭐) are awarded and stored in local progress to keep learning fun and rewarding!

🌟 Summary: Why This Architecture Works for Kids

By combining offline-first verified scientific logic with dynamic voice pitch modulation, multilingual fluency, and transparent thinking cards, the agent transforms a simple query into an exciting, educational discovery adventure!
